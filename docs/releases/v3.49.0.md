# v3.49.0 — local PV-surplus controller for wallboxes

The cloud plan runs every 15 minutes. That is too slow to follow passing
clouds or a kettle switching on, so a wallbox charging "from surplus" either
drew from the grid or exported power it could have used.

## New: the integration follows the surplus itself

For wallboxes with an adjustable charging current, the integration can now
regulate the current locally every 15 seconds. You switch it on per wallbox
in the Crowdergy app (wallbox settings → "PV-Überschuss lokal regeln"); it is
**off by default** and only runs while the wallbox uses the PV-surplus
strategy.

- Surplus is measured at your grid connection: export plus what the wallbox
  already draws. Charging of a home battery that is running on its own
  (passive) counts as well, as long as the battery will still get full
  (state of charge from 80 %).
- The wallbox starts once the surplus has covered its minimum power plus
  0.5 kW for a minute, and stops after the surplus has stayed below the
  minimum for three minutes. It runs at least 5 minutes and pauses at least
  2 minutes, so it does not cycle.
- The current follows the surplus between 6 and 16 A. Phase, current and mode
  are written in the usual order, through the usual safety checks (control
  consent, write limit, entity limits).
- If a measurement is missing or the car is unplugged, it does not start; a
  running charge stops at once.
- If the connection to Crowdergy drops, the controller keeps running — it only
  ever charges from measured surplus, never from the grid.

It ends when the backend sends a regular command for the wallbox, when you
switch it off in the app, when Crowdergy AI is turned off for the device, or
when the backend stops renewing it for an hour while the connection is up. A
charging wallbox is then left in solar mode (or locked, if no solar mode is
mapped).

## Upgrading

Needs the matching backend (already deployed). No entry-data change, no
re-provisioning. Nothing changes until the switch is turned on in the app.
